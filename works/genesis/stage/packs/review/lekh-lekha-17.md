Grammar notes:
- וַיִּקַּח, וַיָּמׇל are wayyiqtol forms: they carry the narrative forward.
- בְּעֶצֶם הַיּוֹם הַזֶּה: עֶצֶם 'bone, substance' is used for 'the very' (same) day.
- The zaqef gadol after זָכָר sets 'every male' apart as a summary of the groups just listed, closed by the etnaḥta on אַבְרָהָם.
- כַּאֲשֶׁר דִּבֶּר links the act to the command of 17:10-14: the obedience is exact.

Translator's note: The Piel perfect דִּבֶּר after a narrative verb is rendered as a past perfect in English ('had spoken'). עֶצֶם is rendered 'very' following Rashi's sense of the same day, not a later one. Parse note: lemma מוּל; the pack lists root מול and no Qal sense number is separate from 'Qal'.

Commentary rulings:
- `rashi:17:23:1` follows: Rashi: 'on that same day on which he was commanded' — by day, not by night; he feared neither the nations nor the scoffers, nor that his enemies would say 'had we seen him we would not have let him circumcise'. The English 'on that very day' follows.
- `rashi:17:23:2` follows: Rashi: וַיָּמׇל is of the pattern וַיִּפְעַל, that is a wayyiqtol form (not a noun or a jussive). The English renders it as a narrative past.
- `radak:17:23:3` not-applicable: Radak: the verse includes both cutting and uncovering (מִילָה וּפְרִיעָה); a legal point beyond the plain wording.
- `radak:17:23:2` follows: Radak: כׇּל־זָכָר means both adults and children, 'every male' without age limit.
- apparatus: Onkelos renders מִקְנַת כַּסְפּוֹ as זְבִינֵי כַסְפֵּיהּ, 'those bought of his silver', as the English does.

- `radak:17:25:2` follows: Radak: this is clearer than the first; אֵת means 'from', as in 'they went out of (אֶת) the city'; the Nifal means 'circumcised in respect of the flesh of his foreskin'.
- apparatus: Radak 17:25:1 notes that Ishmael's age is derived from Abraham being eighty-six at his birth, and that thirteen is the age of obligation.
