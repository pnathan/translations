## Open questions for the editor

The review patched these with its proposed fix; each is open to reversal.

- **bereshit-02 1:6** `commentary:ibn-ezra:1:6:1.relation`: The English 'expanse' (something stretched out) adopts Ibn Ezra's sense of רָקִיעַ, as the translator's own note says; only his identification with the air is left open. 'follows' fits better than 'records'.
- **bereshit-12 3:24** `english`: 'Whirling' commits to one picture of הַמִּתְהַפֶּכֶת. The only normative comment on the word, Ibn Ezra's (filed at 3:23), explains it as a two-edged sword; Rashi says nothing on it. 'Turning' renders the Hithpael participle and leaves both readings (two-edged; brandished to and fro, Radak) to the notes.
- **bereshit-12 3:24** `phrases`: Follows the new English: 'whirling' becomes 'turning'.
- **bereshit-12 3:24** `token:3:24.13.gloss`: Follows the new English.
- **bereshit-12 3:24** `token:3:24.13.note`: The note gives the two readings that the plain 'turning' leaves open.
- **bereshit-12 4:3** `english`: Ibn Ezra, the only normative commentator to speak to it (in his comment at 4:4), holds that יָמִים here is a full year, as in Lev 25:29; Radak allows the same. Rashi is silent. 'After some time' sets aside the normative reading; the proposed English follows it.
- **bereshit-12 4:3** `grammar`: Follows the new English.
- **bereshit-12 4:3** `phrases`: Follows the new English.
- **bereshit-12 4:3** `token:4:3.3.gloss`: Follows the new English; the sense 'days' stands.

- **bereshit-12 4:4** `grammar`: Same antecedent as the token note.
- **bereshit-12 4:4** `token:4:4.7.note`: The feminine plural suffix most naturally takes the feminine plural בְּכֹרוֹת just before it as its antecedent, not the collective צֹאן.
- **bereshit-19 5:24** `commentary:onkelos:5:24:1`: A missing ruling. The Targum's two readings bear on the question of Enoch's death, which the note and Radak's comment discuss, and the variant should be recorded.

- **noach-01 6:12** `token:6:12.1.sense`: Qal 1.a is the first sense of the entry and looks filled mechanically. BDB 1.b is 'see, c. acc. rei', which fits וַיַּרְא ... אֶת־הָאָרֶץ, with the thing seen as the object. Qal 2 (with acc. and a clause of closer definition, as in 1:4) is also arguable, because of the וְהִנֵּה clause.
- **noach-02 6:13** `commentary:rashi:6:13:2`: Rashi's comment is on the words כִּי מָלְאָה הָאָרֶץ חָמָס and identifies the sin as גָּזֵל. That is a reading of what חָמָס means here (Ramban says outright הוּא הַגָּזֵל וְהָעֹשֶׁק), not a homily that leaves the words alone. 'not-applicable' understates it. Ibn Ezra says nothing on the word in this unit, so 'violence' can stand, but the reading should be recorded.
- **noach-05 7:22** `commentary:radak:7:22:1`: A missing ruling. Radak reads the phrase as two parallel bound nouns, not Rashi's chain of three; it bears on the English and is a real alternative to keep.
- **noach-07 8:10** `token:8:10.1.note`: The note should say that the pack's Piel parse is doubtful (Radak: Hiphil) and that Ibn Ezra reads 'began'; proposed wording.
- **noach-09 9:7** `grammar`: The note states as fact a contrast with the shedder of blood in v. 6, which is the midrashic juxtaposition Rashi cites; Ramban takes the plain contrast to be with the creatures of 8:17. Proposed: give both.
- **noach-10 9:10** `token:9:10.17.sense`: Rashi (normative) takes this last חַיַּת הָאָרֶץ as the harmful beasts (המזיקין), which do not go about with people. That is the 'wild animals' sense 1.b, set against the tame 'animal of the earth with you' of 9:10.10. Proposed: 1.b.
- **noach-11 9:19** `token:9:19.8.sense`: The token note itself reads הָאָרֶץ here as the earth's inhabitants. BDB keeps that use (earth = inhabitants of the earth: Gn 6:11; 9:19; 11:1) apart from 1.a, the whole earth as against a part. Proposed: 1.c.
- **noach-11 9:23** `commentary:ibn-ezra:9:23:1.summary`: 'Does not join with both' implies that שְׁכֶם is not bound to שְׁנֵיהֶם, against the morphology. ומלת שכם לא תתחבר most likely explains the singular: שְׁכֶם has no plural in Scripture. Proposed reading above; open to the owner.
- **noach-12 9:24** `token:9:24.7.sense`: עָשָׂה with an object (אֲשֶׁר) and ל of the person is 'do (something) to someone', sense Qal I.1 'do'. Qal I.2 'deal' is the use with an adverb or with עִם/אֵת ('deal kindly with'), which this is not.
- **noach-12 9:27** `commentary:ibn-ezra:9:27:2.summary`: The summary rendered עבד עולם 'a slave forever'. Ibn Ezra's reason, כי מאלה נפצה כל הארץ, makes it 'a slave to the world', to all the peoples descended from these three; Ramban reads Canaan's servitude 'לכל העולם' in the same way.
- **noach-18 11:5** `token:11:5.11.sense`: The translation follows Rashi, for whom הָאָדָם here is the first human individual, not mankind; sense 1 'a man' (an individual human) fits that reading better than sense 2 'man, mankind'. Proposed: 1.
