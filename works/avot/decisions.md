# Pirkei Avot: decisions for every unit

Editorial decisions set before translation. A reviewer may question one in a
`question` patch; the project owner decides.

## Names and titles

- Sages: "Rabbi Eliezer", "Rabban Gamliel", "Hillel", "Shammai". בֶּן: "son of" ("Shimon
  son of Shetach"), except where the name is fixed as one unit (Ben Zoma, Ben Azzai,
  Ben Bag Bag, Ben He He).
- שִׁמְעוֹן הַצַּדִּיק: "Shimon the Righteous". אַנְשֵׁי כְנֶסֶת הַגְּדוֹלָה: "the members of
  the Great Assembly".
- הוּא הָיָה אוֹמֵר: "He used to say".

## Terms

- תּוֹרָה: "Torah". מִצְוָה: "commandment". עֲבֵרָה: "transgression".
- הַמָּקוֹם, of God: "the Omnipresent", with a note on its literal sense, "the Place".
- שָׁמַיִם, of God (לְשֵׁם שָׁמַיִם, מוֹרָא שָׁמַיִם): "Heaven".
- הַקָּדוֹשׁ בָּרוּךְ הוּא: "the Holy One, blessed be He".
- הָעוֹלָם הַבָּא: "the world to come". הָעוֹלָם הַזֶּה: "this world". גֵּיהִנֹּם: "Gehinnom".
- תַּלְמִיד חָכָם: "a disciple of the sages". עַם הָאָרֶץ: "an unlearned person", with a note
  on the literal sense. דֶּרֶךְ אֶרֶץ: by context ("a worldly occupation", "proper
  conduct"), with a note.

## Style

- Plain, exact, modern English. A saying stays a saying: keep its parallelism and its
  brevity; do not expand it into an explanation.
- Quotations from the Bible are translated from the Hebrew of the mishnah. Give the
  source verse in `apparatus`.
- Hillel's Aramaic sayings (1:13, 2:6) and the Aramaic of chapter 5: translate them,
  and say in the grammar notes that they are Aramaic.
