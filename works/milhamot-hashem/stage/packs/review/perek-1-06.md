### Translation under review (1:13)

English: The publisher

Literal: the-publisher

| token | gloss | lex | sense | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1:13.1 | the publisher |  |  | Abbreviation of המוציא לאור (the one who brings out to the light), written with gershayim before the final letter. It is the heading of the publisher's voice, not the author's. |

Phrases:
- 1:13.1 | The publisher | the-publisher

Grammar notes:
- המו"ל is a one-word heading with no verb and no syntax; it names the speaker of the paragraph that follows.
- The abbreviation stands for המוציא לאור, and the English gives the ordinary title 'the publisher'.
- The publisher's voice (1:10 to 1:13) is not the author's, as the decisions for this work require.

Translator's note: Heading only; the section it introduces is translated in its own unit.
