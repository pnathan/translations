## Work

Yichya ben Shelomo Qafich (Sanaa, 1850–1931), *Sefer Milchamot ha-Shem*,
Jerusalem 1931 (5691/2). Composed 1914. Ten chapters (Sefaria: 10 chapters,
225 paragraphs). Against the attribution of the Zohar and against Kabbalah.

## Copyright (US), for the owner to confirm

- Underlying text: published abroad 1931; author died 1931. Israel: life+70,
  long expired; life+50 had also expired (1981) before 1 Jan 1996, so the
  URAA did not restore US copyright. US 95-year term from publication also
  ends 31 Dec 2026, so the 1931 text is public domain in the US by 1 Jan 2027
  at the latest, and probably already. Date of death rests on the PDF's
  biographical note (תר״י–תרצ״ב; 1850–1931); not independently verified.
- Transcriptions: Wikisource text is CC-BY-SA (attribution and share-alike
  attach to the transcription); the 2014/15 PDF's added apparatus is under
  the publisher's ©. A translation made from the 1931 text with the
  transcription as a working copy should record both, and keep the
  edition under a licence compatible with CC-BY-SA, or be made against the
  1931 scan directly.
- Not legal advice.
